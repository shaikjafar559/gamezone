# 🐦 Angry Birds Arcade

> Play online or edit: [https://shaikjafar559.github.io/gamezone/](https://shaikjafar559.github.io/gamezone/)

A retro arcade adaptation of **Angry Birds** built with Microsoft MakeCode Arcade. Use your slingshot, adjust angle and velocity, activate special mid-air abilities, topple destructible wood and stone fortresses, detonate TNT crates, and defeat all the greedy pigs across 4 challenging levels!

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Aim the Slingshot**:
   - `Left` / `Right`: Adjust slingshot pull distance and launch power.
   - `Up` / `Down`: Adjust launch angle and trajectory.
   - White trajectory guide dots indicate where your bird will fly.
2. **Launch & In-Flight Abilities**:
   - Press **(A)** (or Spacebar / Z on keyboard) to **LAUNCH** the bird!
   - Press **(A)** again while in mid-air to trigger your bird's unique power:
     - 🔴 **Red Bird**: Powerful dive-bomb slam!
     - 🟡 **Chuck (Yellow)**: Sonic rocket boost!
3. **Destruction & Strategy**:
   - **Wood Pillars & Beams**: Destructible blocks that topple and collapse under gravity.
   - **Stone Pillars**: Sturdier defenses that require heavier impacts.
   - **TNT Crates**: Direct hits trigger screen-shaking chain reaction explosions!
   - **Pigs**: Pop them with direct hits or crush them under falling building debris!

---

## 🏆 Levels

- **Level 1 — First Flight**: Introduction to slingshot physics with a basic wooden shelter and 2 pigs.
- **Level 2 — Double Trouble**: Twin towers (one stone, one wood) with pigs fortified inside.
- **Level 3 — TNT Blastzone**: Volatile fortress supported by TNT barrels ready for massive explosive chain reactions.
- **Level 4 — Piggy Citadel**: Multi-story stone fortress protected by the armored **King Pig** on his throne!
